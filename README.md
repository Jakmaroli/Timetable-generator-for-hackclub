*Smart Timetable Generator for Students*

StudyPlan AI is a web-based application that helps students generate a personalized study schedule. It uses a custom "Urgency Algorithm" to prioritize subjects based on exam dates and difficulty levels, ensuring you focus on what matters most right now.

*Features:*
*Smart Prioritization:* The "AI" logic automatically prioritizes subjects that have upcoming exams and higher difficulty ratings.

*Dynamic Scheduling:* Generates a day-by-day plan from today until your last exam.

*Pomodoro Style:* Automatically breaks study sessions into 45-minute blocks with 15-minute breaks.

*Customizable Routine:* Set your preferred daily start time (e.g., 09:00 AM) and how many hours you want to study per day.

*Responsive UI:* Clean, modern interface that works on desktop and mobile.
