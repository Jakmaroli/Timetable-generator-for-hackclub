**Smart Timetable Generator for Students**

StudyPlan AI is a web-based application that helps students generate a personalized study schedule.It helps students manage their time. Unlike standard to-do lists, it uses a weighted **Urgency Algorithm** to prioritize subjects based on exam dates and difficulty, ensuring you study the right topic at the right time. The "Urgency Algorithm" prioritize subjects based on exam dates and difficulty levels, ensuring you focus on what matters most right now.

**Features:**

**1.Smart Prioritization:** The "AI" logic automatically prioritizes subjects that have upcoming exams and higher difficulty ratings.

**2.Dynamic Scheduling:** Generates a day-by-day plan from today until your last exam.

**3.Pomodoro Style:** Automatically breaks study sessions into 45-minute blocks with 15-minute breaks.

**4.Customizable Routine:** Set your preferred daily start time (e.g., 09:00 AM) and how many hours you want to study per day.

**5.Responsive UI:** Clean, modern interface that works on desktop and mobile.

**6.Export to Calendar:** Generate a `.ics` file directly in the browser and import your study plan into Google Calendar, Outlook, or Apple Calendar.
